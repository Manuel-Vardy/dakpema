The Initiative

The Problem

I. Digital Literacy Gap:

A pronounced disparity in basic and intermediate digital skills persists between urban southern hubs and northern rural/peri-urban communities in Ghana.

II. Youth Unemployment & Informal Vulnerability:

Over 70% of youth in Tamale operate within the informal economy with limited access to modern tools, e-commerce networks, or financial technologies.

III. Market Trader Exclusion:

Traditional market vendors (majority women) in Tamale markets lack access to digital payment systems, bookkeeping platforms, and broader online supply chains.

IV. Siloed Development Interventions:

Many top-down digital programs fail due to a lack of community ownership, cultural context, and traditional authority endorsement.

The Solution

I. Community-Driven Digital Transformation

II. High youth population + rapid mobile adoption = untapped economic potential.

III. Partnership with the Nyab Dakpema enables deep grassroots access.

IV. Youth technology skills + market trader digital financial inclusion.

THE STAGES OF NDNDI

A multi-stage journey from online registration to incubation and aftercare coaching.

Entrants will be allowed to register online. Follow this link (online registration) to register. Applicants must make sure all information submitted are accurate before clicking APPLY.

At this stage, the best entries are shortlisted. Experts will thoroughly peruse all applications and review applications to verify the authenticity of the information provided at the entry stage is accurate.

The shortlisted entrants will be invited to make a physical presentation to the coaching team. Here, applicants are advised to be more comprehensive, practical, convincing considering there would be a scoring system.

Qualified applicants will be trained by experts in various areas of startups. The entrants will be intensively trained by experienced individuals to get them into the best possible shape to refine their skills to be able to be ready for the market.

This stage means all selected individuals will be provided with what we call aftercare coaching support after graduating from skill-hub. The team at NDNDI will be available to make a recommendation to the winners. NDNDI will provide a start-up school where winners might want to undertake some additional training in particular areas before they go ahead and start their social business.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Candidates must satisfy the following criteria to qualify for the initiative:

  • 1 Applicant resides in Ghana.
  • 2 Applicant must have desire for digitization lessons.
  • 3 Applicant must have the desire to seek support, services, coaching and mentoring from a business incubator.
  • 4 Applicant must have willingness to be held accountable to monthly program goals, receiving and accepting feedback with the capacity to implement or act on suggestions.
  • 5 Applicant must have basic knowledge about online registration.
  • 6 Applicant must be 18-32 years of age.
