About Us

Bridging Tradition and Technology in Northern Ghana

The Nyab Dakpema Naa Digital Initiative (NDNDI) is a flagship community-driven development program designed to bridge the digital divide in Northern Ghana by anchoring modern technology training, digital market integration, and financial inclusion within traditional leadership structures.

Despite rapid national digitalization, youth unemployment and informal sector exclusion remain significant barriers to socio-economic growth across the Northern Region. Working in close partnership with the traditional custodianship of the Tamale Dakpema, NDNDI leverages institutional trust and grassroots mobilization to deliver targeted digital literacy, e-commerce onboarding for market traders, digital entrepreneurship training, and tech-driven cultural heritage preservation. Over 3 years, NDNDI will directly empower 5,000 youth and micro-entrepreneurs, establishing a sustainable model for community-anchored digital transformation across sub-Saharan Africa.

Strategic Objectives

1

Digital Empowerment Hub

Establish a community digital training hub at or near the Dakpema Palace compound, equipped with computers, internet access, and localized learning tools.

2

Inclusive Tech Literacy

Train 3,500 youth in practical digital skills (coding, digital marketing, graphic design, basic data analysis, and cyber hygiene).

3

Market Digitalization (E-Commerce & FinTech)

Onboard 1,500 informal market traders onto mobile money platforms, inventory management tools, and digital marketplaces.

4

Cultural Digitization & Preservation

Archive and project local Dagbon cultural history, festivals, and traditional craftsmanship through digital media channels to foster international culture and tourism.

The Vision

Empowering 5,000 Youth and Traders Through Traditional Authority-Led Digitalization by combining traditional governance with modern digital skills to build an inclusive digital economy in Tamale.
