Partnership

Partnership

The idea assembling initiative is preceded by events such as screening of individuals, incubation hub, mentorship, awards ceremony and coaching. The competition will be open to youth at any level between the ages of 18-32 years.

We invite development agencies, philanthropic organizations, and corporate partners to join hands with the Nyab Dakpema Naa Digital Initiative. Your support will directly fund critical digital infrastructure, bridge the opportunity gap for thousands of young people, and demonstrate how traditional leadership can catalysed sustainable digital development in Sub-Saharan Africa.

Collaborative Channels

How development agencies, philanthropic entities, and corporations partner with NDNDI.

Development Agencies

Anchor regional and international programs directly into traditional authority structures in Tamale, ensuring grassroots community ownership and sustained long-term impact.

Philanthropic Organizations

Underwrite digital scholarships for 3,500 youth, fund solar-powered water infrastructure, and provide seed funding for winners of the idea assembling competition.

Corporate Partners

Equip the Digital Empowerment Hub with computers, provide localized software tools, sponsor market trader onboarding kits, and provide direct mentoring.
